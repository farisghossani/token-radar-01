    Calibration — the multiples, measured on this snapshot

    For every protocol in the snapshot that already records a token, the ratio is its market cap over its TVL. A category's multiple is the median of those ratios; a category with fewer than three observations falls back to the global median. The board's own protocols are absent from this table by construction: they record no token, so they have no ratio.
